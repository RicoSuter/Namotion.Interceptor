# Textures

`grass.png` is our own work, released under CC0 1.0 (https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). It is drawn by `HomeBlaze.Game/tools/make-textures.js`: a pastel fill with a border that draws the 1 m grid. The textures of walls and doors live in `HomeBlaze.Game.Buildings`, under `wwwroot/textures/`.
